The service menu

Everything we love to do.

Tap + on anything that catches your eye. We’ll carry your picks straight into your appointment request.

  • Full Custom Blonding

    Foils placed throughout the entire head for maximum brightness and seamless, blended dimension. Ideal for going noticeably lighter or a big change.

  • Partial Custom Blonding

    Lightness focused around the face, crown and sides while keeping depth underneath. The perfect in-between to keep your blonde fresh.

  • Mini Custom Blonding

    Quick, targeted brightness right along the hairline and part line.

  • Custom Lived-In Lightening

    Soft, sun-kissed dimension with no harsh lines. Balayage-style results that grow out beautifully between visits.

  • Base Color Refresh

    Covers or blends new growth at the root so your shade stays even and polished.

  • All Over Refresh

    One rich, even color from roots to ends, for a full change or a uniform tone.

  • Glaze

    A custom gloss that perfects tone, adds serious shine and kicks brassiness to the curb.

  • Custom Cut

    A cut tailored to your hair type, lifestyle and how you actually style it, starting with a real conversation.

  • Blow It Out

    A relaxing wash followed by a custom blow-dry: sleek and smooth, big and bouncy, or styled your way.

  • Let’s Talk About It

    Not sure what to book? A consultation to talk through your goals before choosing your services.

  • Grow It Out

    Sew-in weft extensions for the length or fullness you’ve been dreaming of. A consultation is required first to color-match and plan your weft count.

  • Move Up

    Weft reset appointment to keep your extensions sitting perfectly. Recommended every 8–12 weeks.

  • Extension Consultation

    The first step to new extensions: we match color, talk goals and map out your install.

  • Volume Lash Extensions

    Lightweight handmade fans on each natural lash for a fuller, darker, show-stopping look.

  • Hybrid Lash Extensions

    The best of both: classic and volume mixed for a textured, wispy finish.

  • Classic Lash Extensions

    One extension per natural lash for effortless length and definition.

  • Lash Lift

    A semi-permanent curl from the root using your own lashes. Pair it with a tint for zero-mascara mornings.

  • Lash Tint

    A custom tint that deepens your natural lash color.

  • Brow Lamination

    Brushes and sets brow hairs into a fuller, fluffier, more defined shape.

  • Customized Facial

    A facial built around your skin, combining professional treatments and targeted products to balance, hydrate and bring back your glow.

  • Facial Waxing

    Clean, precise brow and facial waxing.

  • Brow Tint

    Adds depth and definition to your brows.

Pricing and timing vary by artist and by your hair, so you’ll get an exact quote when we reach out. New extension and major color clients start with a consultation.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What services do you offer?

Hair, skin, lashes and brows: custom color and blonding, cuts and blowouts, sew-in weft extensions, customized facials, lash extensions, lash lifts and tints, brow lamination and more. See the full service menu.

What are your hours?

Meraki is home to a collective of independent professionals, so hours depend on the artist you book with. Send a booking inquiry and your artist will reach out with her availability.

How much do services cost?

Pricing is set by each artist and depends on your hair length, density and goals. Your artist will give you an exact quote before your appointment, and consultations are a great place to start for big changes.

Do I need a consultation for extensions?

Yes. Every new extension client starts with a consultation so we can color-match and plan exactly how many wefts you need. After your install, “Move Up” reset appointments are recommended every 8–12 weeks.

What makes Meraki stand out?

Meraki is more than a salon. It’s a community. Rooted right here in Windham, we pride ourselves on intentional care, genuine connections and creating a space where every guest feels truly seen and valued.

How can I contact you?

Call 207-894-5686, email merakistudiome@gmail.com, or send us a DM on Facebook or Instagram. We’ll get back to you as soon as possible.
